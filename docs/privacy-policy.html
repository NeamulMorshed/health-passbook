Privacy Policy

Omra — Personal Health Tracker  |  Last updated: May 2026

Omra ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal health-tracking application developed for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

Omra is a personal health tracker, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

1. Data We Collect

When you use Omra, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email address, and self-declared user type (patient, doctor, or family member).
  • Health data — vital signs, medication records, meal logs, activity data, and health profile details you enter.
  • Location data — approximate or precise location, only when you grant permission, used for activity tracking.
  • Photos — profile pictures and prescription images you upload; prescription images are processed locally on-device via OCR.
  • Device data — Firebase Cloud Messaging token for push notifications; anonymised crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics (non-debug builds only).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core app features: tracking health records, managing medicines, scheduling appointments, and coordinating care.
  • To send push notifications for medication reminders and appointment updates.
  • To improve app stability using anonymised crash reports.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to any third party.

3. Data Sharing

Your health data is shared only with:

  • Doctors you explicitly connect with through the app.
  • Family members you invite as care companions, limited to permissions you grant.
  • Google / Firebase infrastructure for secure cloud storage, authentication, push notifications, and crash reporting. Firebase's data processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google. Access is controlled by Firestore security rules that restrict each user to only their own data and explicitly connected parties.

5. Sensitive Permissions

  • Location — used only when active; not tracked in the background.
  • Activity Recognition — used to count steps for activity logging; data stays in your personal Firestore document.
  • Camera — used only for profile photos and on-device OCR of prescription labels; images are not stored unless you explicitly save them.
  • Exact Alarms — used to deliver medication reminders at your scheduled times.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your health data from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Omra is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date and notify you via in-app notification.
