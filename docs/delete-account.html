Delete Your Omra Account

How to permanently remove your account and health data

You can delete your Omra account directly from within the app at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

⚠️ Account deletion is irreversible. All your health data — vitals, medicines, appointments, prescriptions, and family connections — will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account (In-App)

1
Open the Omra app Make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
2
Go to Profile Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
3
Open Privacy & Security Scroll down and tap "Privacy & Security".
4
Tap "Delete Account" Scroll to the bottom of the Privacy & Security screen and tap the red "Delete Account" button.
5
Confirm deletion A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap "Delete" to permanently delete your account and all associated data.
✓ Your account and all personal data will be deleted immediately from your device. Remaining data on our servers is permanently removed within 30 days.

Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to access your account to delete it from within the app, contact us by email and we will process the deletion manually within 7 business days.

Email: support@omra.health
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Include: the email address associated with your Omra account.
